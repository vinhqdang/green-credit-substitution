# Bibliography

Verified, DOI-checked reference list compiled for the manuscript on SBFN green banking policy, firm-level access to finance, and green investment/practice adoption using World Bank Enterprise Survey (WBES) data. Every DOI below was confirmed on a real page (publisher site, Crossref API, RePEc/EconPapers, or equivalent) during compilation — none were pattern-guessed. Entries marked "NOT VERIFIED" have no confirmable DOI (institutional report or edited book with no journal/book DOI on record) and carry a note on what was checked instead.
